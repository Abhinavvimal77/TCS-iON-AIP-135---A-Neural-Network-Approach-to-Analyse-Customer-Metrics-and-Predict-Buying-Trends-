The TCS iON Industry Project titled “A Neural Network Approach to Analyse Customer Metrics and Predict Buying Trends” focuses on building an intelligent customer analytics system using a Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) Neural Network implemented through Apache Spark (PySpark). Leveraging a large retail transaction dataset of 100,000 customer records with rich demographic, behavioral, and transactional attributes, the project aimed to predict customer spending behavior by classifying customers into High, Medium, and Low Spenders. A Multilayer Perceptron Classifier was successfully designed and trained using PySpark MLlib, achieving a strong test accuracy of 85.94% and an F1 score of 85.99% after applying feature scaling with StandardScaler. Comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis uncovered actionable insights into purchasing patterns, product preferences, regional performance, loyalty impact, and promotion effectiveness. A complete end to end pipeline was developed, including data preprocessing, feature engineering (StringIndexer, OneHotEncoder, VectorAssembler), model training, hyperparameter experimentation, evaluation, and deployment with model saving and inference. Multiple visualizations such as correlation heatmaps, spending distributions, product category analysis, and prediction probability breakdowns were generated. The business value of this project lies in enabling retail companies to identify high value customers for targeted marketing, optimize inventory planning by product category and region, improve segmentation and loyalty program effectiveness, and reduce marketing budget wastage by focusing efforts on customers most likely to respond. Overall, the project demonstrates a practical application of neural networks in retail analytics, combining strong predictive capability with deep business intelligence to deliver both technical excellence and strategic value.                                                                                                                                                                                                             --ABHINAV M
